Add one-click launcher that auto-downloads the viewer from the iDRAC

idrac.bat/idrac.ps1 replaces the manual setup: it fetches avctKVM.jar
and the native library DLLs directly from the iDRAC (cached per host),
silently installs the bundled JRE 7 to C:\jre if Java is missing, and
launches the console. Optional hosts.txt gives a saved-server menu;
host/user/password can also be passed as arguments for true one-click
shortcuts.
